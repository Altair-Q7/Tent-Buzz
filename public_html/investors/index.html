A PRACTICAL PATH FROM VISION TO OPENING

Fund it. Finish it.
Then grow.

The proposal divides Phase 1 into three stages, with capital released against evidence and clear milestones.

Start with the operating foundations.

Build essential shared infrastructure, open the first new accommodation, and upgrade Tent Buzz in sections where site safety permits.

  • Survey, designs, and approvals
  • Water, power, drainage, and wastewater
  • Six mud-and-stone cottages
  • Restaurant and kitchen
  • Phased Tent Buzz upgrades
  • Access and essential pathways
THE CAPITAL RELEASE GATE

Signed land development and operating rights. Approved Stage 1A bill of quantities and budget. Funds held in a ring-fenced project account.

Build on what the first stage proves.

Add premium accommodation and wellness once operating evidence, demand, and the next funding commitment support expansion.

  • Guest Villa
  • Ayurvedic Retreat Centre
  • Natural pool conversion
  • Spices outlet and processing area
  • Gym
  • Core guest amenities
THE CAPITAL RELEASE GATE

Review Stage 1A booking and operating data. Appoint the Ayurvedic Medical Director. Validate wellness demand and pricing. Secure additional funding.

Connect the wider destination.

Expand aviation facilities and the farm ecosystem after the commercial arrangements and earlier-stage performance are established.

  • Helipad lighting and upgrades
  • Customer lounge
  • Pilot accommodation and fire safety
  • Biogas facilities
  • Expanded farm and aquaponics
  • Biodiversity and optional amenities
THE CAPITAL RELEASE GATE

A signed Chipsan Aviation agreement defining fees, charges, commission basis, and safety responsibilities. Review Stage 1B performance before committing capital.

Stage contents are proposed and depend on completed cost plans. If a stage exceeds secured funding, items move to a later stage. Opening dates will be set after funding close, design, approvals, and contractor scheduling.

A MODEL TO DISCUSS, NOT A PROMISE

What sits behind
these numbers?

The proposal’s lower-to-upper subtotal is approximately ₹1.57–₹1.90 crore in annual gross revenue. It is a planning scenario for future operating inventory, before expenses.

  • ↗One saleable Guest Villa and six cottages, available for all 365 days. The separate Ayurvedic Retreat Centre is not a saleable guest villa in this model.
  • ↗Room rates and 60–75% occupancy are management assumptions requiring demand validation. Phased openings or closures reduce available room nights.
  • ↗Tent Buzz assumes 12 paying guests × 8 days per month × 12 months × ₹2,000. The rate basis and inclusions remain to be confirmed.

ADDITIONAL REVENUE STREAMS ARE EXCLUDED

Ayurveda, aviation, restaurant, café, retail, and farm sales are excluded from this subtotal. Their volumes, prices, contracts, and costs need separate validation. Bundled packages must avoid counting the same room or meal twice.

Gross revenue is not profit or distributable cash. The proposal does not establish validated EBITDA, net profit, investor IRR, dividend forecasts, or exit valuation. Payroll, commissions, utilities, maintenance, taxes, financing, working capital, and other operating costs still need a complete financial model. Source: proposal Appendix D, sections D2–D8; model dated 10 November 2026.

THE QUESTIONS THAT MATTER

Good partnerships
start with clarity.

Is ₹2.5 crore the total Phase 1 project cost?+

No. It is the proposed initial funding requirement. The total cost depends on approved designs, engineering, detailed bills of quantities, supplier quotations, pre-opening costs, contingency, and working capital.

What is operating, and what is proposed?+

Tent Buzz is described as an existing operating campsite. The proposal also records existing helipad and plantation infrastructure. The premium accommodation, retreat centre, and wider facility portfolio are proposed developments or upgrades. Supporting records, permissions, and commercial agreements must be reviewed during due diligence.

Does an investment include ownership of the land?+

The proposal states that the land is owned by the project partner. Landowner consideration and operating rights remain to be agreed. No land ownership or fixed operating-company share is implied by an investment amount.

Are the revenue scenarios guaranteed returns?+

No. The model shows illustrative gross revenue from stated assumptions. It does not establish profit, cash distributions, investor IRR, or a guaranteed exit. A complete operating and cash-flow model needs validation before investment decisions.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

A little clarity
before you camp.

Still curious? Just drop us a WhatsApp.

How do I book my stay?+

All bookings happen directly on WhatsApp at +91 7012549782. Send us your dates and group size, and our team will confirm availability, current rates, inclusions, and payment arrangements.

Do I need to bring my own tent?+

We provide standard tents for two, sleeping bags, and basic camping equipment. Bring personal essentials, a warm layer, and suitable footwear. Ask our team about anything specific you would like to bring.

What about bathrooms and showers?+

Separate shared toilet and bathing facilities are available within the compound. This is an outdoor camping stay with shared facilities.

Are meals or a campfire included?+

Ask us on WhatsApp for the food arrangements and inclusions for your dates. We have designated campfire and BBQ spots; arrangements depend on conditions and must be confirmed with the team.

Can we come as a group or with family?+

Families, couples, solo travellers, and groups are welcome. The current practical arrangement is around nine two-person tents. Message us with your group size so we can discuss the right setup and availability.

When can I visit?+

Tent Buzz operates throughout the year. The hills change with the seasons, so confirm availability, weather conditions, check-in and check-out times, and what to pack with our team before travelling.
